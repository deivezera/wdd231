Discover Curitiba

The Green City of Brazil

Curitiba is the capital of Paraná and the economic heart of southern Brazil. Long praised for urban planning and its network of parks, it blends a strong business community with a rich cultural and natural landscape. For prospective members and visitors, the city offers a compact, walkable centre surrounded by green space and opportunity.

  • 1768 Year founded
  • ~1.8M City population
  • ~3.7M Metro population
  • 430 km² Municipal area
