NP Membership Benefits

No fee

  • Access to the online member directory
  • Invitations to community networking events
  • Monthly chamber newsletter
  • Eligibility for volunteer and community programs

Bronze Membership Benefits

From R$ 300 / year

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Business listed in the member directory
  • Discounted tickets to chamber training sessions
  • Access to quarterly networking mixers

Silver Membership Benefits

From R$ 750 / year

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Advertising rotation as a home page spotlight
  • 10% discount on chamber events and workshops
  • Free admission to member-only training series
  • Priority registration for Chamber Luncheons

Gold Membership Benefits

From R$ 1,500 / year

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Premium spotlight placement on the home page
  • 20% discount on all chamber events and training
  • Featured logo in the annual Chamber directory
  • Complimentary booth at the annual business expo
  • Dedicated account support from Chamber staff
