Tibian Tech • Website planning document

Built to help small businesses grow with clear technology and steady support.

The Tibian Tech website will present a friendly, professional partner for website creation, data systems, AI setup, and recurring care without the jargon or over-engineering.

See the wireframes

Site name

Tibian Tech

This name is direct, memorable, and rooted in the company’s identity while keeping the focus on modern technology support.

  • Primary audience: SMB owners and operators
  • Primary CTA: Book a free consultation
  • Theme: trustworthy, modern, practical

Site purpose

Show how Tibian Tech turns tech complexity into calm, useful momentum.

What the site will do

The site will introduce Tibian Tech as a practical technology partner for businesses that need better websites, smoother operations, and guidance with AI and data.

What visitors will find

Visitors will find clear service descriptions, a simple process, trust signals, and a clear next step to book a consultation.

How success will be measured

The site will be judged by conversion focus, accessibility, mobile readiness, confidence-building content, and a consistent user journey.

Audience scenarios

These questions guide the structure of every page.

“I need a better website, but I do not want a huge project.”

The homepage and services overview will explain quick wins, right-sized website packages, and the path to launch.

“I want AI help, but I am not sure where to start.”

The AI and business systems sections will present approachable solutions with plain-language guidance and clear next steps.

“I need someone dependable to keep things running.”

Recurring services, support language, and the “talk → build → grow” process will reinforce confidence and long-term partnership.

Visual system

Color and type choices support a professional, trustworthy brand.

Color scheme

Primary#546498
Secondary#C8925B
Text#111827
Background#F4F4F6

The deep blue will anchor navigation and call-to-action areas, while the warm metallic accent highlights important steps and cards.

Typography

Inter Bold — headings, hero statements, and navigation labels

Inter Regular — body copy, service descriptions, and supporting text

Inter was chosen because it reads clearly on screens and supports a modern but calm interface for business audiences.
